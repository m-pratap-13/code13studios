1. Introduction

Welcome to Code 13 Studios. This Privacy Policy describes how developer Pratap Mondal ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, protects, and discloses personal information when you use our mobile applications distributed via Google Play Store and our website.

We are firmly committed to user privacy. Our development philosophy is built around minimal data collection: we only collect or process data strictly required to make the app function effectively for you. When an app can operate completely offline without internet connectivity, we deliberately choose not to connect to external servers.

2. Applications Covered by this Policy

This privacy center covers all active applications published by Code 13 Studios on Google Play Store:

  • My Tuition Fees (Package: com.code13studios.mytuitionfees) — Offline student fee and batch management tool.
  • Next Hair Cut (Package: com.code13studios.nexthaircut) — Men's salon appointment booking & queue manager.
  • Abacus : Mental Math (Package: com.code13studios.abacusmentalmath) — Mental arithmetic training & virtual abacus.
  • Offline Arena (Package: com.code13studios.offlinearena) — 100% offline casual puzzles & arcade games.

3. Core Privacy Principles

  • Zero Data Brokering: We never sell personal information to advertising networks, data aggregators, or third-party marketing companies.
  • Data Minimization: We do not ask for permissions that the app does not actively need. For example, Offline Arena requests zero permissions.
  • Local-First Storage: Where possible, data (such as student names and fee amounts in My Tuition Fees) is saved strictly in local SQLite/Room storage on your device.
  • User Consent: Permissions such as location or notifications are requested at runtime only when you initiate the relevant action.

4. Third-Party Services and SDKs

To provide stable application updates, error monitoring, and standard Google Play functionality, our apps may interact with trusted Google Play services:

  • Google Play Services: Used for in-app updates, licensing verification, and core Android operating system integration.
  • Google Play Core: Facilitates seamless app asset delivery and updates without collecting personal records.
🛡️
Third-Party Compliance Guarantee

All third-party libraries integrated into our applications comply strictly with the Google Play Developer Program Policy and Family Policy guidelines.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

Our applications do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). In games and educational tools intended for general audiences, we implement age-appropriate safeguards and adhere to the Google Play Families Policy.

6. Your Rights & Data Deletion Requests

Regardless of your geographic jurisdiction (including GDPR in Europe and CCPA/CPRA in California), you have full rights to:

  • Request confirmation of what data is collected.
  • Request deletion of any personal data stored on our servers.
  • Opt out of optional notifications or revoke device permissions at any time via your Android System Settings.

To request permanent account or data deletion, please contact us at contact.pratap13mondal@gmail.com.

My Tuition Fees — Privacy Policy

Package: com.code13studios.mytuitionfees
Education Google Play ↗
My Tuition Fees Privacy Matrix
Student Records 100% Stored on Your Phone
Internet Required No (Fully Offline Capable)
Sensitive Data Shared None (Zero External Transmission)

1. Purpose of the Application

My Tuition Fees is designed as a productivity and organizational assistant for private tutors, coaching institutes, and teachers to track student enrollment, batch timings, tuition fees, monthly payments, and attendance.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

When you use My Tuition Fees, you may input information including:

  • Student names, contact phone numbers, and enrolled batch/subject names.
  • Monthly fee amounts, payment transaction dates, and outstanding balance status.
  • Attendance marks and notes.
🔒
Strict Local Storage Architecture

All student lists, fee records, and attendance logs are saved exclusively in your phone's internal application database. Code 13 Studios has no access to your student names, fee amounts, or coaching records. They are never uploaded to our servers.

3. Android Permissions Requested & Why

Permission Purpose Required vs Optional
READ / WRITE STORAGE Allows exporting PDF payment receipts or backup files to your device storage. Optional (only used when exporting/importing).
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Sends local reminders when student fees are due or unpaid. Optional (can be toggled in app settings).

4. Data Deletion and Backup Control

Because your data resides on your physical phone, you have total control over it:

  • You can edit or permanently delete individual students or payment logs at any time from within the app.
  • Uninstalling the app or clearing the app storage from Android settings will immediately and permanently wipe all local database files.

Next Hair Cut — Privacy Policy

Package: com.code13studios.nexthaircut
Lifestyle Google Play ↗
Next Hair Cut Privacy Matrix
Location Access Approximate/Nearby Salons Only
Background Tracking Never Tracked in Background
Booking Data Direct with Chosen Salon

1. Purpose of the Application

Next Hair Cut is a lifestyle application designed to help men discover barbershops, view available time slots, make haircut and grooming appointments, and avoid waiting in long salon queues.

2. Information We Process

  • Contact Information: Your name and mobile phone number provided when booking an appointment to ensure the salon can identify and confirm your reservation.
  • Booking History: Selected salon, chosen barber, appointment date/time, and requested grooming services.
  • Device Location: Used solely in the foreground to locate barbershops near you on the map.

3. Location Data Disclosure

Next Hair Cut requests foreground location access (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) strictly to calculate distances to nearby barbershops. We do not track your location in the background, we do not log your movement history, and we do not sell location telemetry to any third party.

4. Notifications

We use notification permissions (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) to send you important alerts regarding your queue status, appointment confirmations, and reminders so you never miss your booked slot.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

You can delete your booking history or request full removal of your phone number from salon records by emailing contact.pratap13mondal@gmail.com.

1. Complete Offline Guarantee

Offline Arena is built from the ground up to be enjoyed anywhere without an internet connection. It contains casual puzzles, arcade challenges, and brain games that function 100% autonomously on your mobile device.

🎮
Zero Personal Data Collection Policy

Offline Arena does NOT collect, store, transmit, or monitor your name, email, phone number, contacts, location, hardware identifiers, or browsing activity. There are no tracking scripts, user profiling engines, or external telemetry databases.

2. Game Progress & High Scores

Game levels unlocked, high score records, and in-game settings are saved exclusively in your device's local application sandbox. If you clear the app data or uninstall the app, this progress is reset locally and no residue exists on any server.

3. Children & Families Policy Compliant

Offline Arena is suitable for audiences of all ages, including children. Because it does not gather any personal data or transmit personal information over the internet, it is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy requirements.
