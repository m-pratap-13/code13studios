1. Introduction

Welcome to Abacus: Mental Math ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy details our practices concerning the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

By installing or using Abacus: Mental Math, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We respect user confidentiality and do not require personal registration:

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, log in, or provide personal details such as your full name, email, phone number, or home address.
  • No Sensitive Personal Data: We do not collect or store sensitive personal information such as biometric credentials, payment card details, or national IDs.

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To deliver a seamless offline practice experience, the App stores your arithmetic progress and custom settings exclusively on your device using local storage:

  • Scores & Progress: Practice high scores, daily streaks, level progression, problem completion counts, and accuracy metrics.
  • User Preferences: Abacus themes, bead color styles, sound volumes, and haptic feedback toggles.
💾
Data Sovereignty & Local Control

All game data remains strictly on your local device. It is never uploaded to our servers. Uninstalling the app or clearing its app data will erase this saved progress.

4. Advertisements & Third-Party Advertising Services

To keep Abacus: Mental Math free for all learners and support ongoing improvements, third-party advertisements are displayed in the App.

Third-Party Ad Networks (e.g., Google AdMob)

We partner with third-party advertising providers such as Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). In order to serve ads, these services may automatically collect non-personally identifiable diagnostic and technical device information, including:

  • Device Identifiers: Advertising ID (such as Google Advertising ID / GAID on Android, or IDFA on iOS) and hardware identifiers.
  • Device & Network Data: IP address, device model and manufacturer, operating system version, network connection type, and regional/language settings.
  • Ad Interaction Data: Ad views, clicks, impressions, and performance diagnostic metrics.

These partners use this data to:

  • Deliver contextually appropriate or personalized advertisements (based on your device settings and local consent requirements).
  • Limit repetitive ads (frequency capping).
  • Detect fraud, click manipulation, and invalid traffic.
  • Analyze and monitor ad performance.

Please review the privacy policies of our third-party advertising partners:

  • Google Privacy Policy ↗
  • Google Advertising Technologies & Policies ↗

5. How to Control or Opt Out of Personalized Ads

You can manage or disable interest-based advertising directly from your device settings:

  • Android: Go to Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads) and select Delete advertising ID or turn on Opt out of Ads Personalization.
  • iOS: Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking and turn off tracking permissions.

Note: Opting out of personalized ads does not turn off ads. You will continue to see non-targeted, contextual advertisements.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Families Policy)

Abacus: Mental Math is built as an educational arithmetic tool suitable for students and family audiences.

  • We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions).
  • Any integrated ad networks are set to comply with Google Play's Families Policy and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), ensuring ads served to child audiences are family-safe and non-personalized.
  • If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately for prompt deletion.

7. Device Permissions

The application only requests essential standard permissions necessary for advertising delivery and touch feedback:

Permission Usage Reason
INTERNET Enables the App to communicate with advertising networks to load and display ads.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Allows the App to detect active network connectivity before requesting ad content.
VIBRATE Provides tactile haptic feedback during interactive bead movement on the abacus.

8. Data Security and Deletion

Your arithmetic practice data never leaves your device. You can permanently delete all local game data at any time by clearing the application storage via device settings or by uninstalling the application.

9. Rights under GDPR and CCPA/CPRA

Depending on your region (such as the European Union or California), you retain statutory privacy rights including the right to access, request deletion, and opt out of data sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising via your device settings.

10. Updates to This Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or legal requirements. Updated policies will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date".
