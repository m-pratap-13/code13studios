Privacy Policy for Arrow Escape

Arrow Escape • Minimalist Puzzle Game

Developer: Pratap Mondal
Studio: Code13 Studios
Effective Date: November 20, 2026
Last Updated: November 20, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Arrow Escape ("the Game", "the Application", "we", "us", or "our"), developed and published by Pratap Mondal under Code13 Studios. We are committed to protecting the privacy, security, and personal data of our users ("player", "user", or "you").

This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user information, what data may be processed, how it is used, and what rights you have regarding your information. By downloading, installing, or playing Arrow Escape, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

2. Offline-First Architecture & Data Collection

Offline-First Philosophy: Arrow Escape is designed to be fully playable without requiring an active internet connection. We do not require you to register an account, log in with an email address, or provide any personal identification to play the game.

A. Information You Do NOT Provide:

  • We do not collect your real name, physical address, or phone number.
  • We do not require user registration, passwords, or personal login accounts.
  • We do not access your device contacts, camera, microphone, or photo library.
  • We do not collect or store credit card numbers, bank account numbers, or billing data.

B. Information Stored Locally on Your Device:

The Application uses an embedded local database (SQLite via expo-sqlite) saved exclusively on your local device storage. This data includes:

  • Game level progression and unlock states (Levels 1 through 500).
  • Stars earned per level (1, 2, or 3 stars) and completion times.
  • Remaining player lives and hints count.
  • Career statistics (total moves made, levels completed).
  • Audio settings (sound effects toggle, haptics toggle, background music volume).

Note: All this data resides on your device. We do not maintain any central servers or external databases where your individual puzzle progression is mirrored or uploaded.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

To keep Arrow Escape free to play, the Application integrates industry-standard third-party services that may automatically collect certain diagnostic and non-personally identifiable information:

Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK)

The Game displays non-intrusive advertisements provided by Google AdMob, including banner ads and optional rewarded ads (which grant players extra hints, lives, or stars upon voluntary viewing).

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and process certain device information, including:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID on Android) or Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA on iOS).
  • Device Technical Information: Device make and model, operating system version, screen resolution, mobile carrier, and language settings.
  • Coarse Location: Generalized location derived from IP address (country or city level) to serve relevant regional ads.
  • Ad Interaction & Telemetry: Ad impressions, click activity, video completion events, and fraud prevention telemetry.

For more information on how Google collects and uses your data, please review Google's official Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

4. How We Use Collected Information

Any data processed through the Application or third-party SDKs is used solely for the following legitimate purposes:

  • Core Gameplay Delivery: Ensuring levels, stars, records, and sound preferences are remembered across gaming sessions.
  • Ad Serving & Monetization: Providing banner ads and voluntary rewarded video ads so that the game remains completely free to enjoy.
  • Security & Anti-Fraud: Detecting click fraud, automated bots, and protecting the integrity of the game and advertising ecosystem.
  • Performance Optimization: Identifying crashes, memory leaks, and optimizing puzzle responsiveness.

5. Advertising Opt-Out & User Controls

You have full control over interest-based advertising on your mobile device:

On Android:

  1. Open your device's Settings app.
  2. Navigate to Google > Ads (or Privacy > Ads).
  3. Tap Delete advertising ID or Reset advertising ID.
  4. You can also enable Opt out of Ads Personalization if supported on your Android version.

On iOS:

  1. Open your device's Settings app.
  2. Navigate to Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising and toggle off Personalized Ads.
  3. Navigate to Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking to manage app tracking permissions.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Arrow Escape is a family-friendly puzzle game suitable for general audiences of all ages. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • We do not knowingly collect, request, or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the EEA/UK).
  • The Game does not offer open chat rooms, social messaging, or public user profile features.
  • Advertisements delivered in child-directed or family modes comply with Google AdMob's Families Policy and do not use behavioral profiling.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has inadvertently provided personal information to us, please contact us at support.code13.pratap@gmail.com so we can promptly investigate and delete any such data.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Because your puzzle game progress is stored strictly inside your device's local application sandbox, you retain complete ownership over your data at all times:

  • Clearing Data: You can clear all game data and restart progression at any time by going to your phone's Settings > Apps > Arrow Escape > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data.
  • Uninstalling the App: Uninstalling Arrow Escape permanently removes the SQLite database and all associated local preferences from your device.

8. Security Measures

We value your trust in using Arrow Escape. Because the Game operates offline-first and does not transmit personal data across unencrypted public networks, your game progression is kept safe inside your device's operating system sandbox.

9. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Depending on your geographic location, you may have specific privacy rights:

  • Right to Know / Access: You have the right to know what personal data is processed.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): You have the right to delete your data (achieved by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app).
  • No Sale of Personal Data: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to data brokers or third parties for monetary consideration.
